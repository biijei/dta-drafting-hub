# How DTA Drafting Hub works

A client does not pay when they send a request. They send the details and files first. DTA reviews them, sets the price, and emails a PayPal link. Work starts only after that payment is confirmed.

1. The client chooses a service, fills in the form, and uploads any files. On the last step they press Submit request. Nothing is charged on that page.

2. The request is saved as Pending Review. The client gets an email with their Request ID and a "Track your request" button. They can also open Track My Request and enter that ID plus the email they used. The business inbox gets a different email. It names the client and the service and asks DTA to review the files, set the price, and send the PayPal link. That email has a "Set the price" button. It opens that request in the admin panel. If the person is not signed in, they sign in and land on the same request.

3. An owner opens the request, reads the files, and types the USD price. Generate and email link sends the client an email with the amount and a "Pay with PayPal" button. The request becomes Awaiting Payment. The same link is on the Payment tab of the client’s request. Resend link sends that same link again. Changing the price before payment sends a new link and replaces the old one. Specialists can see the price. Only an owner can set it or send the link. Drafts cannot be uploaded yet.

4. The client pays with PayPal. When PayPal confirms the payment, the request becomes Submitted. The client gets an email that payment was received and work has started, with an "Open your request" button. The business inbox gets a different email: payment is confirmed, so start the work or assign it to a specialist. That email has an "Open the request" button.

5. Staff can assign the request and mark it In Progress. When a draft is uploaded, the request becomes Awaiting Your Review. The client gets an email that the draft is ready, with an "Open your request" button. They download the draft and either approve it or ask for changes.

6. If they ask for changes, the request becomes Revision Requested. The business inbox gets an email with the client’s note and an "Open the request" button. Staff update the draft and upload it again. The client is emailed when that new draft is ready, with an "Open your request" button, and they can approve it or ask for another change.

7. If they approve, the request becomes Approved. Staff then upload the final document. The request becomes Completed. The client gets an email that the document is complete, with an "Open your request" button, and can download it from the request page.

Business emails go to the primary email saved in Admin Settings. The client’s emails always go to the email address on the request.
